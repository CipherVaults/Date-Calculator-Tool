Adds years, months, weeks and days to a date, or subtracts them. Month ends clamp (Jan 31 + 1 month = Feb 28/29).
Years and months step on the calendar first; then weeks count as business weeks (weeks × working days per week), then business days.

    PRIVACY & RUNTIME
    ● 100% Offline ● Zero Telemetry ● On-Device Engine

    All date calculations run 100% on-device with zero telemetry. History and settings are saved locally on this device only and can be cleared anytime. Optional GitHub Releases update checks can be toggled in Settings.

    STATED LIMITS
    • Islamic holidays are estimated from the tabular Hijri calendar and can differ from the announced dates by 1–2 days.
    • UK one-off bank holidays are verified through 2027.
    • Holidays that Egypt moves by decree are not modelled; add them as custom holidays.
    • Month ends clamp: Jan 31 + 1 month = Feb 28/29, so Jan 31 + 1 month − 1 month = Jan 28.
    • Holiday presets cover 1900–2199; dates run from 0001-01-01 to 9999-12-31.
